📋 Educational Disclaimer

Important Notice:

NutriScope Pro is an educational nutrition tracking tool designed for personal wellness monitoring and informational purposes only. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Key Points:
• All nutritional data is approximate and sourced from public databases
• Individual nutritional needs vary based on health status, medications, and medical conditions
• Consult a healthcare provider or registered dietitian before making significant dietary changes
• Do not use this app for medical decision-making without professional guidance
• Individuals with eating disorders or medical conditions should seek professional help
• Nutritional recommendations are general and not personalized medical advice

Data Accuracy:
Nutritional values are sourced from USDA FoodData Central, Indian Food Composition Tables, and published research. Values may vary based on food preparation methods and regional variations.

Use at Your Own Risk:
By using NutriScope Pro, you acknowledge that you use it at your own risk and release the creators from any liability.

📚 Nutrition Data Sources

Primary Sources:
• USDA FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov)
• WHO/FAO Food Composition Database
• Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) Food Composition Tables
• Nutritive Value of Indian Foods (Gopalan et al.)

Nutritional Guidelines:
• Recommended Dietary Allowances (RDA) by ICMR
• WHO Guidelines on Micronutrient Deficiencies
• Mifflin-St Jeor Formula for BMR Calculation

Referenced Standards:
• Daily Value (DV) percentages based on FDA guidelines
• Activity multipliers from Harris-Benedict and Roza equations
• Macronutrient ratios from American Heart Association

Disclaimer:
Food composition values are approximations. Actual values vary based on cultivation methods, processing, preparation, and regional factors.
